**Sample.** Twelve synthetic windows (906 tabs written for this evaluation, 54 to 200 tabs each), usually two repeats per setting. It separates settings that are far apart; it does not rank settings within a few points of each other, and it says nothing about real browsing.

**Phase 1 bar.** Mean applied F1 on all tabs of at least 0.75 over the twelve windows, and p90 latency within 6 seconds on the windows of up to 66 tabs (the small and middle bands below; a failed request counts as over budget). Recordings made with the pilot wording or a stub, and partial runs, are not judged.
Ten windows means all but dev and life, the pair that drafted the pilot prompt; the current prompt's example name ("Wedding Plans") is checked by `eval/check.mjs` against every gold task name and tab title.

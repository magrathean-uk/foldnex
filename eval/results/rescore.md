# Phase 0 results under one scoring policy

Generated by `node eval/rescore.mjs <models dir>` from the fixtures, `results/local.json`, `results/ablation.json` and the recorded answers in `results/provider/full/`. No model was called. The policy lives in `eval/scoring.mjs`.

## Policy

- Applied result: Each tab in at most one group (first placement wins, unknown ids ignored). Omitted tabs and tabs in a group named "Review Later" (any case) are unassigned. At most ten groups, the Review Later pool counting as one when any tab is unassigned: the largest groups stay (ties by first appearance), the tabs of the rest are unassigned.
- Tasked: Pairwise F1 over pairs of tabs with a gold task; unassigned tabs are singletons. All tabs: Pairwise F1 over every tab; a tab without a gold task is a gold singleton; unassigned tabs are singletons.
- Recorded: As the original reports scored it: plan answers as given (first placement wins, omitted ids singletons, any number of folders), local clusters as the ten largest clusters of 2+ tabs, tasked tabs only.
- Ten windows: Every window except dev and life, which were used to draft the plan prompt. The plan prompt's example folder name is one of `life`'s gold groups, so those two windows are not clean for the model rows.
- Local rows: rules and gold categories are single fixed runs; the cluster rows use the settings chosen leave-one-window-out (title words: tau 0.02 in eleven folds, 0.03 in one; 4M: idf, wl 1, tau 0.2 in every fold). Model rows: mean over two recorded attempts per window.

Offline Smart Clustering (Zero-AI / Enterprise Air-Gapped)

Offline mode is active. Tabs are grouped using local URL structure and title similarity.

Why use Offline Smart Mode?

  • Enterprise-Safe: Complies with strict corporate DLP policies. No data ever leaves your device.
  • Air-Gapped & Firewalled: Functions with zero internet access, zero Chrome flags, and zero external AI dependencies.
  • Zero Cost: Completely free forever without any API keys or subscriptions.
  • Automatic Fallback: Even when you choose Gemini Nano or Cloud APIs, Foldnex will automatically fall back to this engine if your network or policy blocks AI.
